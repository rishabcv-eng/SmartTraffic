# Impact model

A controller that reports "12% lower average queue" is hard for a transport
department to act on. The same result expressed in litres, tonnes and rupees is
directly comparable against the cost of installing the system.

This document publishes every constant used, so a reviewer can challenge the
arithmetic rather than having to trust it. The implementation is
`backend/app/services/impact.py`, and the assumptions are overridable per
request via the `assumptions` field on `POST /api/impact/compare`.

## Time base

| Quantity | Value | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 tick | 5 seconds | The engine discharges up to 5 vehicles per approach per tick. At a saturation flow of ~1800 veh/h/lane over two lanes that is ~1 veh/s, so 5 vehicles takes 5 seconds. |

Every delay figure in the project is measured in ticks. This is the only
conversion between simulation time and wall-clock time, and changing it scales
all impact figures linearly.

## Fuel and emissions

| Constant | Value | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Car idle consumption | 0.70 L/h | Typical petrol hatchback/sedan idling. |
| Bus idle consumption | 2.50 L/h | Diesel bus, engine running while stationary. |
| Petrol CO₂ | 2.31 kg/L | Standard combustion factor. |
| Diesel CO₂ | 2.68 kg/L | Standard combustion factor. |

Only **idling** emissions are modelled. Cruise and acceleration emissions are
not, which makes these figures conservative: real stop-start driving emits more
than idling alone, so a controller that removes stops saves more than this
model credits it with.

## Money

| Constant | Value | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Petrol price | ₹105/L | Adjust to local pump price. |
| Diesel price | ₹95/L | Adjust to local pump price. |
| Value of time | ₹120 per person-hour | Mid-range for Indian urban commuters. This is the single most contestable number here; halve it and the rupee headline roughly halves. |

## Occupancy

| Vehicle | People | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Car | 1.5 | Used for person-delay weighting. |
| Two-wheeler | 1.2 | Present in the model, not yet generated by the mock engine. |
| Bus | 35 | Drives the transit-priority controller's objective. |

Person-delay is what makes bus priority defensible: a bus stuck for one minute
costs roughly 23 times more human time than a car stuck for one minute.

## Annualisation

| Constant | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Peak-equivalent hours per day | 14 |
| Operating days per year | 330 |

A benchmark run covers a few minutes of simulated time. The annual figure scales
that measured period up to 14 hours a day, 330 days a year. This is the
assumption most likely to overstate results, because it treats the whole
operating day as though it looked like the measured period. Treat annual
numbers as an order-of-magnitude indication, not a forecast.

## Scope

All figures cover the four modelled junctions only. Scaling to a whole city
requires per-junction demand calibration — see `POST /api/calibrate` and
`docs/BENCHMARKS.md`.

## Worked example

Pooled over five seeds, 180 ticks, normal demand, `mpc-lite-v1` against the
fixed-time baseline, the model currently reports roughly:

- ~124,000 litres of idle fuel avoided per year
- ~296 tonnes of CO₂ avoided per year
- ~₹8.1 crore of fuel and time cost avoided per year

Regenerate with:

```bash
curl -X POST localhost:8000/api/impact/compare \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"scenario":"normal"}'
```

These come from the mock engine. Final claims must be regenerated in SUMO.
